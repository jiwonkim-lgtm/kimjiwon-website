1. Motivation & Problem Definition

Maritime industries continuously seek methods to reduce fuel consumption and improve propulsion efficiency. Rotor sails provide an eco-friendly propulsion technology using the Magnus effect generated by rotating cylinders. This project investigates how operating conditions such as angular velocity, wind speed, and geometry affect rotor sail efficiency and vibration reduction.

2. Research Methods

Experimental Conditions

• Rotor angular velocity control
• Wind speed variation experiments
• Rotor geometry comparison
• Observation of vibration characteristics

Physics Principles

• Magnus Effect analysis
• Fluid dynamics interpretation
• Force and thrust efficiency comparison
• Experimental data observation

3. Experiments & Results

Key Findings

• Rotor angular velocity strongly affected thrust efficiency.
• Increased wind speed changed propulsion performance trends.
• Rotor geometry influenced vibration and stability.
• Rotor sails demonstrated potential for sustainable ship propulsion.

4. Discussion & Applications

This study suggests that rotor sails may contribute to environmentally sustainable marine transportation. Potential applications include:

• Eco-friendly marine propulsion systems
• Fuel consumption reduction technologies
• Sustainable shipping engineering
• Marine fluid dynamics optimization
