1. Motivation & Problem Definition

Ultrasonic technology plays an important role in robotics, sensing systems, acoustic engineering, and medical imaging. However, conventional ultrasonic structures have limitations in directional control and focusing efficiency. This project investigates whether a dolphin melon-inspired biomimetic structure can improve ultrasonic wave focusing and directional precision.

2. Research Methods

Experimental Method

• Designed biomimetic melon-inspired geometry
• Constructed ultrasonic focusing experiment
• Compared propagation characteristics
• Measured focusing and directional performance

Simulation Method

• FDTD simulation method
• Acoustic wave propagation analysis
• Impedance comparison
• Experimental validation

3. Experiments & Results

Key Findings

• Biomimetic melon structure improved ultrasonic focusing efficiency.
• Experimental and simulation trends showed similar behavior.
• Energy concentration near focal regions increased.
• Potential improvement in directional precision was observed.

4. Discussion & Applications

This research suggests that dolphin-inspired biomimetic structures may improve ultrasonic control systems. Potential applications include:

• Robotic sensing systems
• Medical ultrasound technology
• Acoustic wave control systems
• Physical AI sensing hardware
• Intelligent scientific instrumentation
